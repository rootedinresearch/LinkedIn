After more than 20 years leading operations and innovation across higher education, municipal partnerships, and small business environments, I’ve learned that meaningful progress happens when strategy, technology, and execution come together to improve how people learn and work.
In my current work, I focus on applying AI in practical, real-world settings—building tools that improve both learning and operational performance. I have developed an AI-enabled sales training bot that prepares teams for cold, warm, and ready-to-buy lead intake, along with an AI-powered CSR assistant that adapts sales conversations based on customer inquiries while guiding consistent outcomes. I have also created a sales event training tool to prepare agents for in-person lead capture based on customer personas, as well as internal applications that allow business operations to be managed efficiently from a mobile-first environment.
In parallel, I am building a project that blends horticultural science with generational knowledge to help backyard gardeners improve yields—reflecting my broader approach to innovation: combining structured knowledge with practical application to create tools people will actually use.
I build within the Microsoft, Google, and OpenAI ecosystems, focusing on simple, scalable solutions that drive adoption. I document this work through a building-in-public portfolio to share both progress and lessons learned: https://bit.ly/script-app-exec
What stands out to me about this role is the emphasis on applied AI within a learning environment. My approach aligns closely: start with the user, design systems that support them, and ensure adoption through clarity, training, and consistency.
Throughout my career, I have worked across operations, CRM systems, marketing, and partnerships—translating ideas into scalable programs that deliver measurable outcomes. I am particularly energized by the opportunity to help students build confidence using AI and CRM tools in ways that prepare them for modern business environments.
I would welcome the opportunity to bring a practical, execution-focused approach to innovation at TCU—helping the Sales Center continue to grow its reputation while delivering meaningful value to students and partners.
