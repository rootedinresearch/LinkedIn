# 📌 Project 2: AI-Enabled Predictive Parking Map & Live Availability Engine

- **Role / Title:** Executive Sponsor & CS Student Mentor
- **Focus Domain:** Applied AI / Predictive Analytics / Software Engineering Mentorship / Cost Optimization
- **Organization:** University of Texas at Arlington (Auxiliary Services / Campus Mobility)
- **Primary Objective:** Replace a recurring $65,000/year third-party commercial SaaS expenditure with an in-house, zero-cost AI predictive parking availability platform.

---

### 🛑 Problem Solved
* **$65,000 Annual Vendor SaaS Drain:** The enterprise was paying an outside software vendor $65,000 annually for parking occupancy tracking and map display software.
* **Driver Uncertainty & Lot Congestion:** Commuters and visitors lacked predictive foresight on lot fill-rates, leading to circling, localized traffic bottlenecks, and missed classes or meetings during peak morning rush hours.
* **Vendor Dependency:** Reliance on third-party SaaS limited custom feature development, data ownership, and real-time integration with internal campus systems.

---

### 💡 The Solution
Mentored and led a student engineering team of **UTA Computer Science Interns** through the architecture and deployment of an in-house platform:
1. **Live REST API Integration:** Streams real-time occupancy sensor and permit transaction data across all campus parking facilities.
2. **Predictive Availability Modeling:** Machine learning and trend forecasting algorithms predict lot saturation curves up to 60 minutes in advance.
3. **Automated Backup Routing:** If a primary facility is projected to fill, the platform dynamically recommends open secondary lots based on proximity and permit tier.

---

### 📈 Business Impact & Measurable Outcomes
* **$65,000 Recurring OpEx Eliminated:** Replaced the external commercial software contract with a permanent, zero-cost proprietary system ($325,000+ 5-year net savings).
* **Proactive Commute Management:** Predictive backup recommendations eliminate campus lot congestion and reduce peak-hour circling for 44,000+ students and 5,000 staff.
* **Complete Campus Data Ownership:** Retained 100% of campus occupancy telemetry in-house for operational planning and academic transportation research.
* **Student Engineering Mentorship:** Mentored undergraduate and graduate Computer Science students through production-grade REST API architecture, algorithmic testing, and live institutional deployment.

# 📌 Project 3: Interactive Event Parking Logistics & Wayfinding HTML Builder

- **Role / Title:** Executive Sponsor & Product Architect
- **Focus Domain:** Workflow Automation / Event Logistics / Zero-Cost Web Tooling / Operational Efficiency
- **Organization:** University of Texas at Arlington (Auxiliary Services / Campus Mobility)
- **Primary Objective:** Eliminate manual back-and-forth event parking planning and deliver automated, interactive wayfinding packages for 500,000+ annual campus event visitors using a lightweight HTML tool with **zero ongoing costs**.

---

### 🛑 Problem Solved
* **Severe Administrative Overhead:** Event coordinators and administrative staff spent dozens of hours per week manually assigning parking lots, looking up distances, and emailing PDFs back and forth for hundreds of campus events annually.
* **Costly Software Alternatives:** Enterprise event-mapping and spatial navigation SaaS solutions required steep annual recurring subscriptions and complex backend infrastructure.
* **Guest Confusion & Event Congestion:** Visitors attending athletic games, conferences, concerts, and graduation ceremonies often parked in unauthorized lots or arrived late due to uncoordinated walking distances.

---

### 💡 The Solution
Mentored a team of **UTA Computer Science Interns** to build a standalone, browser-based event coordination HTML page with **zero ongoing costs**:
1. **Zero-Cost Standalone HTML Architecture:** Operates entirely client-side as an interactive HTML page requiring zero recurring hosting fees, server maintenance, or software subscriptions.
2. **Dynamic Visual Lot & Venue Selector:** Event planners click on venue destinations and available parking zones on an interactive campus map.
3. **Predictive Travel & Walking Engine:** Automatically calculates recommended arrival windows, precise walking itineraries, and parking difficulty ratings based on expected event attendance and historical lot data.
4. **1-Click Digital Wayfinding Generator:** Instantly outputs polished HTML wayfinding packages, digital invitations, and web-ready maps that event organizers can email directly to attendees.

---

### 📈 Business Impact & Measurable Outcomes
* **Zero Ongoing Software or Infrastructure Costs:** Engineered as a self-contained, client-side HTML tool with $0 in recurring vendor licensing, hosting, or maintenance expenses.
* **95% Reduction in Administrative Overhead:** Reduced the time required to plan and publish event parking logistics from several hours down to under 5 minutes per event.
* **Elevated Guest Experience & Communication:** Delivered clear, customized arrival itineraries for over 500,000 annual event visitors across commencements, athletic events, and academic conferences.
* **Decreased Operational Burden:** Slashed event-day front-desk phone inquiries and parking enforcement confusion by providing pre-cleared, transparent parking directions.
